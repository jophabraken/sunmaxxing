Brunch in Neukölln

Neukölln's brunch scene mixes Middle Eastern flavours, third-wave coffee, and casual courtyard seating — most spots open from 9 and run till early afternoon.

Neukölln brunch is its own genre — less polished than Mitte, more international than Prenzlauer Berg, with strong Middle Eastern and Anatolian influence from the neighbourhood's demographic. The two prime stretches are the Schillerkiez (around Herrfurthplatz) and the Reuterkiez (around Maybachufer and Hobrechtstrasse). Both have good outdoor seating; the Schillerkiez side gets the most reliable sun thanks to the open Tempelhofer Feld nearby.

Neukölln cafés are generally walk-in only and queue management is informal. Arriving before 11:00 will save you a wait at the popular spots. Most cafés serve brunch through to 15:00 or 16:00 on weekends.

01

Hallmann & Klee

Böhmische Straße 13, 12055 Berlin · set-menu brunch · south-east courtyard

Neukölln's most celebrated brunch — a set menu (no à la carte) that changes monthly. Booking essential. The small outdoor courtyard catches morning sun. Vegetarian-leaning, beautifully plated, leisurely-paced. The €40 set menu is the move.

Sing Blackbird

Sanderstraße 11, 12047 Berlin · vintage shop + café · east-open terrace

A vintage-clothing store and café in one. The outdoor terrace catches reliable morning sun. Brunch is informal — eggs, sourdough, granola, a daily special. Friendly, sceney, and Sunday-morning slow.

Concierge Coffee

Paul-Lincke-Ufer 39, 10999 Berlin · canal-side specialty café · west-open

Technically Kreuzberg-Neukölln border, sitting directly on the Paul-Lincke-Ufer. The terrace faces the Landwehrkanal; west-open so afternoon sun is reliable. Coffee-forward menu — pastries, toasts, simple egg dishes.

Kefir

Substantial Turkish breakfast — cheeses, olives, sucuk, simit, eggs, honey, jams. The outdoor terrace catches solid mid-afternoon sun. Larger groups work well here; expect leftovers.

Frequently asked

Do you need to book Hallmann & Klee for brunch?

Yes — reservation strongly recommended, often booked out 2–3 weeks in advance for weekend slots. They run a single seating per service. The format is a set menu, no walk-ins for tables.

What's a good Turkish brunch in Neukölln?

Kefir is the standout — substantial Anatolian breakfast platter with cheeses, olives, sucuk, simit, and jams. Cafés along Sonnenallee also serve generous Turkish breakfasts at lower prices but with smaller outdoor seating.

Is brunch in Neukölln expensive?

Mostly mid-priced for Berlin. A typical plate-with-coffee runs €12–16. Hallmann & Klee's set menu is €40 per person, which is the high end of the neighbourhood. Most casual brunches stay under €18 per person.

Which Neukölln brunch has the best outdoor sun?

Concierge Coffee's canal-side terrace is the most reliably sunny, catching west-open afternoon light. Kefir's south-west terrace gets the longest stretch. Sing Blackbird catches morning sun then goes into shade by 12:30.
