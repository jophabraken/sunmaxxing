Find the sun in Berlin

Sunmaxxing is a free, live map of every sunny terrace, rooftop, beer garden, and brunch spot in Berlin. Every minute, we calculate which of Berlin's 2,800+ outdoor venues are currently in direct sunlight — using OpenStreetMap building geometry, the sun's exact position, and the current weather. No app install. No signup. Just open the map and find a sunny seat.

Where to sunmaxx in Berlin right now

Berlin's outdoor scene clusters into a handful of clearly defined types — and each one catches the sun differently. Rooftop bars like Klunkerkranich, Monkey Bar, and Hotel Amano sit above the city's shadow line and stay sunny long after the streets go dark. Beer gardens (Biergärten) are the city's most reliable summer institution — Café am Neuen See, Prater, and Schleusenkrug all run from midday well into the evening, with most of their tables in full sun until at least 19:00 in June and July.

If you're after breakfast in the sun, brunch spots with outdoor seating across Kreuzberg, Mitte, and Prenzlauer Berg open from around 9 a.m. and catch the morning light. And for golden hour, the best sunset spots in Berlin — the canal at the Oberbaumbrücke, the hill at Viktoriapark, the dome at Tempelhofer Feld — face west and are made for slowing down at the end of the day.

How Sunmaxxing tracks the sun

Each venue on the map is annotated with whether it's currently in direct sunlight, based on three inputs: the building geometry around it (from OpenStreetMap), the sun's exact azimuth and altitude right now (computed with SunCalc), and the current cloud cover at that location (from Open-Meteo). The result updates every minute on the map and ahead of time — drag the time slider to see exactly when a terrace falls into shadow later today. Full technical explainer: how Sunmaxxing works.

Frequently asked questions

When does the sun set in Berlin today?

Berlin's sunset time changes a lot through the year — from around 16:00 in mid-December to nearly 21:30 in late June. Current daily sunrise and sunset times for Berlin, plus the best places to watch them, live at Berlin sunrise & sunset times.

What's the difference between a Biergarten and a Sonnenterrasse?

A Biergarten is the Bavarian tradition — usually shaded by old chestnut trees, often serving traditional German food, culturally made for long, lazy sessions. A Sonnenterrasse (sun terrace) is any outdoor terrace deliberately oriented to catch the sun — a café terrace facing south, a rooftop bar, the deck of a Spree-side restaurant. Sunmaxxing maps both, and you can filter between them using the chips at the top of the map.

Why is a terrace I know to be sunny showing as in shadow?

Two common reasons. First, cloud cover: when Open-Meteo reports overcast conditions, no terrace is in direct sun even if the geometry would otherwise allow it. Second, geometry gaps: OpenStreetMap building outlines are crowdsourced and occasionally miss a small structure (a kiosk, a pergola, a temporary stage) that's casting real shadow. The data improves every night.

Does Sunmaxxing work outside Berlin?

Right now Sunmaxxing covers Berlin only — the inner ring of the city including Mitte, Kreuzberg, Prenzlauer Berg, Friedrichshain, Neukölln, and Charlottenburg, stretching out to Tempelhofer Feld and along the Spree. Expanding to other cities is on the roadmap.

Is Sunmaxxing free?

Yes. Sunmaxxing is free, has no ads, no account, and no app to install. It runs entirely in your browser.

Biergärten sind Berlins zuverlässigste Sommerinstitution — Café am Neuen See, Prater und Schleusenkrug laufen vom Mittag bis spät in den Abend, und die meisten Tische stehen im Juni und Juli bis mindestens 19 Uhr in voller Sonne.

Häufig gestellte Fragen

Wann geht heute in Berlin die Sonne unter?

Berlins Sonnenuntergang variiert stark übers Jahr — von etwa 16:00 Uhr Mitte Dezember bis kurz vor 21:30 Uhr Ende Juni. Aktuelle Sonnenauf- und -untergangszeiten für Berlin, samt der besten Aussichtspunkte: Berlin sunrise & sunset times.

Was ist der Unterschied zwischen einem Biergarten und einer Sonnenterrasse?

Ein Biergarten ist eine bayerische Tradition — meistens unter alten Kastanien, mit deutscher Küche, gemacht für lange Sommerabende. Eine Sonnenterrasse ist jede Außenterrasse, die bewusst auf Sonne ausgerichtet ist — Café-Terrasse nach Süden, Dachbar, Spree-Restaurant. Sunmaxxing zeigt beides; per Filter-Chip oben auf der Karte kannst du zwischen den Kategorien wechseln.

Warum zeigt mir eine bekannte sonnige Terrasse Schatten an?

Zwei Gründe sind häufig. Erstens Bewölkung: wenn Open-Meteo bedeckten Himmel meldet, ist keine Terrasse in direkter Sonne, auch wenn die Geometrie sie zuließe. Zweitens Lücken in den Daten: OpenStreetMap-Gebäudeumrisse sind crowdsourced und übersehen manchmal kleinere Strukturen (Kiosk, Pergola, temporäre Bühne), die echten Schatten werfen. Die Daten verbessern sich täglich.
